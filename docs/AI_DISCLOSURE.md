# AI use disclosure

The Space Apps Challenge asks participants to say where and how AI tools were used, and to acknowledge AI-generated images.

## Logo (AI-generated image)

- The Umbral logo (`umbral-logo.png`, `umbral-logo-v2.png`) was generated with an AI image tool from my description.
- `public/logo.svg` is a vector redrawing of `umbral-logo-v2.png` for the web app (same design and colours; the crop rows are redrawn as simple shapes). `public/logo-mark.svg` and `public/favicon.svg` are crops of that vector without the word mark.
- The app shows a visible credit, "Logo generated with an AI image tool", in the footer, in "Sources and methods" (About), and in the exported summary.
- No AI-generated image is used as data. Maps, satellite images and charts come from the cited sources.

## Code, research and documentation

I used AI assistants (large language models) to search the literature, write and review code, and draft documentation. I defined the problem, chose the data, assumptions and scope, and checked the outputs against the cited sources. Agronomic validation with local growers or INTA is still pending.

## In the app

The "Questions about this example" panel does not use an AI model. Its answers are computed with the same rules as the rest of the app.
